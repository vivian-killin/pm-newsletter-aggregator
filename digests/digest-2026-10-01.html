Teresa Torres

Generating Opportunity Solution Trees with AI: How Vistaly Rebuilt Its Product Around Interview Synthesis, Evals, and Repair Loops
TL;DR — PM TakeawayAI's real power for product discovery isn't automation—it's synthesis. Vistaly redesigned its product to let AI extract patterns from customer interviews, then built repair loops so teams could validate and correct the output. This shift from "AI does the work" to "AI structures the thinking" is a template for AI-native product architecture.
Read more

In this episode, Torres and Matt O'Neill discuss how Vistaly moved beyond using AI as a simple tool and instead made AI-driven synthesis central to how the product organizes customer research. The key insight: opportunity solution trees (a core PM framework) become more powerful when AI processes raw interview data and surfaces patterns humans would miss.

The critical move was adding evaluation and repair loops. AI generates candidate trees, but humans decide which insights are valid. This creates a feedback mechanism that trains both the AI and the team's intuition. For PMs, this reframes AI not as a replacement for thinking, but as a partner that speeds up the grind of qualitative analysis.

This piece examines how the most successful AI systems have typically won by scaling simple algorithms rather than encoding expert knowledge. The "dot" represents narrow, optimized expertise; the "swarm" represents distributed, data-driven learning at scale. The lesson for PMs: systems that seem less intelligent upfront often outperform bespoke solutions as volume grows.
