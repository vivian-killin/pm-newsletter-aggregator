When teams ship faster via AI, they often discover they lack the GTM bandwidth, pricing strategy, or customer targeting to scale adoption. Worth your time regardless of when it was published
Why it's worth your timeGilad argues that tracking dev productivity via output metrics (KLOCs, commits, tokens spent, story points) is a trap. Instead, evaluate initiatives by outcome and impact, and use product discovery to reduce wasted output before scaling throughput.
Read more

Gilad's viral LinkedIn cartoon sparked conversation about how to measure development productivity in an AI-accelerated world. His takeaway: common metrics like tokens spent, lines of code, or velocity are not meaningless, but they miss the point. A PM's job is to measure whether the work moves the product forward, not whether it's being produced faster. This shift is especially critical now that AI tools can generate massive amounts of code—garbage at scale is still garbage.
