Torres has moved beyond her foundational evals guide to release the working tool itself. The harness solves the practical problem of establishing baselines: you need to run every input through your prompt, save each output, then measure results against a rubric. Why companies are becoming a series of loops | Anish Acharya (a16z)
TL;DR — PM TakeawayAcharya argues that AI is reshaping how companies operate: every function—from product to operations to support—becomes a loop where users get feedback, iterate, and improve. PMs need to stop thinking in waterfall terms and design products as continuous feedback systems.
Read more

In this 79-minute conversation, Acharya (a consumer investing partner at a16z and former GM at Credit Karma) reframes company structure around loops rather than departments. When AI agents and users interact repeatedly—each cycle tightening the result—the traditional org chart becomes less relevant than the feedback mechanisms you build in.

Acharya touches on why fears of a "permanent underclass" from AI are overblown, the rising importance of distribution as a moat (not feature parity), and the next big consumer opportunity: "/loop, make me happier"—where the system learns your preferences over time. For PMs, this means prioritizing feedback velocity and measurable improvement cycles over big launches.
